# [L4D & 2] Fix Replace Max HP

### Introduction
- Fix max health not restored after replacements.
	1. Bot/Player replacements.
	2. Player to player replacements (`ZombieManager::ReplaceTank`).

<hr>

### Requirement
- **DHooks**

<hr>

### Installation
1. Put **plugins/l4d_fix_replace_maxhp.smx** to your _plugins_ folder.
2. Put **gamedata/l4d_fix_replace_maxhp.txt** to your _gamedata_ folder.
